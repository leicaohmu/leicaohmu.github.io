About Us

Lei Laboratory, established in 2023, focuses on developing and applying advanced biostatistical methods and artificial intelligence techniques to solve critical problems in medical research. Our interdisciplinary team combines expertise in statistics, computer science, and medicine to advance precision medicine and improve patient outcomes.
