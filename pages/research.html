Research Areas

Our laboratory focuses on developing and applying cutting-edge biostatistical methods and artificial intelligence techniques to address critical challenges in medical research and clinical practice.

📊

Biostatistical Methodology

Development of novel statistical methods for analyzing complex biomedical data, including survival analysis, longitudinal data analysis, and causal inference.

🤖

Machine Learning in Medicine

Applying machine learning and deep learning techniques for disease prediction, diagnosis, and prognosis using multi-modal medical data.

🧬

Genomics and Precision Medicine

Integrative analysis of genomic, transcriptomic, and clinical data to identify biomarkers and develop personalized treatment strategies.

🏥

Clinical Data Science

Mining electronic health records and real-world data to generate clinical evidence and improve healthcare decision-making.

🖼️

Medical Image Analysis

Developing AI algorithms for automated medical image segmentation, classification, and quantitative analysis.

💊

Drug Discovery and Development

Statistical design and analysis of clinical trials, pharmacokinetics/pharmacodynamics modeling, and drug repurposing using AI.

Current Projects

AI-based Early Cancer Detection

Developing machine learning models for early detection of multiple cancer types using multi-omics data and imaging biomarkers.

Funding: National Natural Science Foundation of China

Biostatistical Methods for High-Dimensional Data

Developing novel statistical methods for variable selection, dimension reduction, and inference in high-dimensional biomedical studies.

Precision Medicine for Cardiovascular Diseases

Integrating genomic, clinical, and lifestyle data to develop personalized risk prediction models and treatment strategies for cardiovascular diseases.

Collaborations

We actively collaborate with leading research institutions and hospitals both nationally and internationally, including medical schools, cancer centers, and biotechnology companies.
