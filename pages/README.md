# Lei Laboratory Website

Official website for Lei Laboratory at Harbin Medical University.

🌐 **Website**: https://leicaohmu.github.io

## 🏥 About

Lei Laboratory focuses on Biostatistics and AI Methods in Medicine. Established in 2023, our mission is to advance precision medicine through innovative statistical and computational approaches.

**Principal Investigator**: Lei Cao, Ph.D., Associate Researcher  
**Email**: caolei@hrbmu.edu.cn  
**Institution**: Harbin Medical University

## 🎯 Research Focus

- Biostatistical Methods Development
- Machine Learning in Clinical Research
- AI-driven Medical Data Analysis
- Precision Medicine Applications

## 👥 Team

- **Current Students**: 14 (5 PhD, 9 Master)
- **Alumni**: 6 (1 PhD, 5 Master)

## 🌐 Website Structure

- **Home**: Laboratory overview and latest news
- **Members**: Current students and alumni
- **Research**: Research areas and projects
- **Publications**: Published papers and achievements
- **News**: Laboratory updates and events
- **Courses**: Course information and seminars
- **Resources**: Open and restricted research resources
- **Join Us**: Recruitment information
