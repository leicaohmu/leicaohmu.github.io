Join Us

We are always looking for motivated and talented individuals to join our team. If you are passionate about biostatistics, machine learning, and their applications in medicine, we would love to hear from you!

🎓 Open Positions

PhD Students

Positions: 2-3 per year

Requirements:

  • Master's degree in Statistics, Biostatistics, Computer Science, Mathematics, or related fields
  • Strong mathematical and programming skills (R, Python)
  • Research experience in biostatistics or machine learning (preferred)
  • Good English reading and writing abilities

Master's Students

Positions: 3-4 per year

Requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Medicine, or related fields
  • Interest in biostatistics and medical data analysis
  • Basic programming skills
  • Strong motivation for research

Postdoctoral Fellows

Positions: 1-2

Requirements:

  • PhD degree in Biostatistics, Statistics, Bioinformatics, or related fields
  • Strong publication record in peer-reviewed journals
  • Expertise in statistical methods or machine learning
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively

📧 How to Apply

If you are interested in joining our laboratory, please send the following materials to Dr. Lei Cao at caolei@hrbmu.edu.cn:

  • CV/Resume with detailed education background and research experience
  • Cover letter describing your research interests and career goals
  • Transcripts (for students)
  • Representative publications or manuscripts (if available)
  • Contact information for 2-3 references

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Early application is encouraged.
